# CareQueue problems and benefits

Figures marked (estimate) are reasoned assumptions to be replaced with pilot data. Sourced figures link to [04 market research](04-market-research.md).

## Owner doctor / clinic owner

| Problem | CareQueue solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crowded waiting room, arguments about turn, staff shouting token numbers | QR token, live queue on patients' phones, "call next" on staff app | Calmer clinic; patients wait outside or at home | Activation |
| Long waits are the top complaint in reviews; Indian OPD studies show waits far longer than consultations | Live wait estimate and alerts | Fewer complaints, better Google rating | Acquisition |
| Patients do not return for follow-up; chronic patients drift to other doctors | Practitioner-set follow-up reminders | More follow-up visits, better outcomes | Retention, revenue |
| Paper prescriptions lost; patients call for "what was the medicine?" | e-Rx sent as secure WhatsApp link with registration number | Fewer calls; professional image | Retention |
| Receptionist on phone all day answering "how long?" | Patient sees wait on phone | Receptionist time freed for billing and care | Activation |
| Cash leakage and no daily collection report | UPI links and daily summary | Owner trusts collections | Revenue |
| Fear of data breaches and new DPDP law | Consent records, encryption, access logs, deletion workflow built in | Compliance by default, less legal anxiety | Retention |
| ABDM pressure and incentives, but software looks complex | ABHA verify and record linking (P1) inside the same front-desk flow | Eligible for DHIS incentives (₹20 per qualifying transaction above base level) | Revenue |

## Receptionist and staff

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Manual register, handwriting errors | Patient self-registers on own phone | Faster check-in, clean data |
| Elderly and emergency patients need priority | Priority flag with reason in staff app | Fair, visible ordering |
| Lab staff handle "is my report ready?" calls | Automatic WhatsApp when ready | Fewer calls and visits |
| No-shows on appointments | 24 h and 2 h reminders | Fewer empty slots |

## Patient (and pet parent)

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Waits 1–2 hours in a crowded room with other sick people | Token on phone; can wait outside or at home | Less waiting in crowd, less infection exposure |
| Does not know when turn will come | Live position and "3 ahead" / "your turn" alerts | Predictability |
| Loses paper prescription or report | Secure link on WhatsApp, always retrievable during validity | Convenience |
| Has to travel back to collect lab report | Report link on WhatsApp | Saves a trip |
| Forgets follow-up or vaccination date | Reminder set by doctor/vet | Better continuity of care |
| Worried about privacy of health data | Clear notice, consent choices, opt-out, OTP-protected links | Trust |
| Wants ABHA-linked records | ABHA verification and record linking (P1) | Records available across providers with consent |

## The pitch in numbers (estimate)

GP clinic, 70 patients a day, ₹400 fee:

- If follow-up reminders bring back 3 extra follow-up visits a day that would otherwise lapse (estimate), that is 3 × ₹400 × 25 days = **₹30,000 a month** versus ₹1,499 subscription and about ₹600 WhatsApp credits.
- Receptionist saves about 1 hour a day of phone calls (estimate).
- 10–20 more Google reviews a month (estimate) improves local ranking for "doctor near me".

Sell the calm waiting room; the follow-up revenue pays for it.
