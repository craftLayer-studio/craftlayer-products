### Serviceable market for CareQueue (estimate)

- Assume 35% of 13.86 lakh allopathic doctors run or co-run a private OPD clinic, and an average of 1.5 doctors per clinic: ≈ 3.2 lakh clinics (estimate).
- Add dental, physio, small labs/collection centres and vet clinics: + 1.5 lakh (estimate, no reliable public count).
- Assume 30% are in Tier 1–3 urban areas with 30+ patients a day and smartphone-using patients: ≈ 1.4 lakh serviceable clinics (estimate).
- At ₹1,600 blended ARPU (see 05): SAM ≈ ₹270 crore a year (≈ USD 32 million) (estimate).
- Year-3 target of 2,500 clinics ≈ 1.8% of SAM ≈ ₹4.8 crore ARR (estimate).

### Pricing benchmark summary

- Small-clinic EMR software in India: **₹499–₹1,999 per month** per doctor/user, often billed annually.
- Government floor: **₹299 per month** effective (eSushrut@Clinic) for records.
- Global per-practitioner tools: $59–$89 (≈ ₹5,000–₹7,500, estimate at ₹85/USD).

CareQueue at ₹699 (Token) / ₹1,499 (Clinic) / ₹2,999 (Clinic Plus) flat per clinic sits in the Indian band and below global tools. It complements rather than replaces EMRs, so a clinic can run CareQueue beside Practo Ray or eSushrut.

## Differentiation

| Dimension | Typical clinic EMR | CareQueue |
|---|---|---|
| Lead feature | Doctor's EMR and prescription pad | Patient waiting experience: QR token, live wait on phone |
| Who sees value first | Doctor (after learning curve) | Patients and receptionist on day one |
| Patient channel | SMS or app download | WhatsApp, no app |
| Data sharing | PDF attachments common | OTP-protected expiring links |
| Fit | Replace current workflow | Works alongside any EMR or paper |
| Labs and vets | Separate products | Same product, module switch |

## Trends 2024–2026

1. **ABDM scale.** 93+ crore ABHA accounts and 104+ crore linked records by July 2026 ([News On AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/over-104-crore-health-records-linked-to-over-93-crore-abha-accounts-says-govt/)). Scan and Share at registration counters is mainstream in public hospitals.
2. **Government digitising small clinics.** eSushrut@Clinic launched June 2026; 800+ facilities onboarded at launch ([PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2278996)).
3. **DHIS incentives.** ₹20 per additional ABHA-linked transaction for hospitals/clinics above base level (later corrigendum: 100 transactions/month base); labs ₹15; software vendors (DSCs) get 25% of the facility's incentive ([ABDM DHIS policy](https://abdm.gov.in:8081/uploads/Financial_Incentive_Policy_DHIS_e96a62fd28.pdf), [Corrigendum](https://abdm.gov.in:8081/uploads/Corrigendum_to_Digital_Health_Incentive_Scheme_vf_e4f1ca0b19.pdf)). Check current base levels before selling on it.
4. **WhatsApp per-message pricing** from 1 July 2025; India utility ≈ ₹0.11–₹0.12, marketing ≈ ₹0.86 from 1 January 2026; service replies within 24 h free; INR billing mandatory by 31 December 2026 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing), [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline)). CareQueue messages are mostly utility, so cost per clinic is low.
5. **Telemedicine growth.** IMARC (cited by a vendor) puts India telemedicine at USD 3.1 billion in 2024 rising to USD 19.9 billion by 2033 ([MocDoc blog](https://mocdoc.com/blog/why-indian-clinics-are-moving-to-digital-clinical-management-systems)). P2 opportunity.
