# 02 Problems and benefits — ClassNest

Each row: the problem today, how ClassNest solves it, the benefit, and the metric it moves.

## Owner

| Problem | ClassNest solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fee defaulters: 10–25% of monthly fees arrive late or never (estimate, from owner interviews to be validated in pilots) | Instalment plans, UPI links, reminders at D-3/D0/D+3/D+7, defaulter dashboard | Fees arrive earlier; owner stops making awkward calls | Revenue |
| Fees collected in cash and UPI to personal numbers, hard to reconcile | Single ledger: every payment, cash or UPI, gets a numbered receipt | Clean books for GST and income tax | Revenue |
| Admission-season leads on paper slips and missed calls | Poster QR, demo booking, pipeline with auto follow-up | More admissions from the same flyer spend | Acquisition |
| No proof for parents that classes happen and the child attends | Attendance alerts and monthly report cards | Parent trust; fewer drop-outs mid-year | Retention |
| Teacher payout disputes | Sessions taught come from attendance; payout sheet auto-built | Fewer arguments; faster month-end | Retention |
| New coaching-centre rules (registration, minimum age, refunds) create paperwork | Compliance pack: age check at admission, refund calculator, fee display page, student counts | Ready when the state inspector asks | Retention |
| Owner has no numbers (collection %, attendance %, conversion) | Owner dashboard on the phone | Decisions on which batch to add or close | Retention |

## Staff (front desk and teachers)

| Problem | ClassNest solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper roll-call takes 5–10 minutes per batch (estimate) | Entrance QR or one-tap list | Class starts on time | Activation |
| Parents call to ask about attendance and fees | Parents get alerts and receipts automatically | Fewer calls | Retention |
| Notes and homework lost in large WhatsApp groups | Notes per batch with a permanent link | Less re-sharing | Retention |
| Marks in notebooks, rank done by hand | Marks entry with auto rank and report card | Hours saved after each test | Retention |

## Parent (end customer who pays)

| Problem | ClassNest solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learns about bunked classes weeks later | Absence alert within 15 minutes | Peace of mind; acts the same day | Retention |
| Forgets due dates; fee receipts lost | Reminders with a UPI link; receipt on WhatsApp | Pays in 30 seconds; has proof | Revenue |
| No visibility of progress | Report card with trend and rank | Sees value for money; renews next year | Retention, referral |
| Hard to compare institutes during admissions | Demo booking page with courses, fees, faculty, timings | Easier decision | Acquisition |
| Worried about child's data being misused | Clear consent screen, no ads, opt-out any time | Trust | Retention |

## Student

| Problem | ClassNest solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chases notes and test scores | Notes and scores per batch | Studies more, chases less | Retention |
| Schedule changes announced late | Timetable change alerts | Fewer wasted trips | Retention |

## Why the owner pays (one sentence)

If ClassNest recovers even one defaulted monthly fee of ₹1,500 per month, Starter (₹799) pays for itself; the absence alerts make parents stay for the full year.
