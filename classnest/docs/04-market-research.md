The gap between ₹58,000 crore and the GST-implied ₹30,652 crore suggests a large untaxed, cash-heavy segment. That segment is our ICP: small centres that need a ledger and digital receipts.

### Positioning

ClassNest is the offline coaching centre's front desk: attendance-to-parent alerts, fee recovery and admission leads. It is not an LMS or a course store. Price sits below Classplus and at the TutorBird level in USD.

## Trends 2024–2026

1. **Regulation of coaching centres.** Ministry of Education guidelines (January 2024) set model rules for states: registration, no enrolment below 16, no misleading promises, pro-rata refund within 10 days, published fees and refund policy ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/no-student-below-16-rank-guarantee-performance-based-batches-public-results-norms-for-coaching-centres-moe), [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/education/new-guidelines-for-coaching-institutes-education-ministry)). Compliance tooling becomes a selling point.
2. **Children's data rules.** DPDP Rules 2025 notified on 13–14 November 2025; Rule 10 requires verifiable parental consent; most obligations start 18 months after notification ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/), [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified-309928)).
3. **WhatsApp per-message pricing.** Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered template; India marketing went from ₹0.7846 to ₹0.8631 in 2026; utility about ₹0.115 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). Products must favour utility templates.
4. **UPI everywhere and AutoPay.** UPI AutoPay supports recurring debits; standard no-AFA limit is ₹15,000 per transaction ([Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-autopay/), [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/master-recurring-payments-upi-autopay-guide/)).
5. **Hybrid learning settles.** Online coaching is a smaller slice (USD 510.1 million in 2025 per [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-online-coaching-market)) than institutes (USD 7.2 billion), so offline remains the core.
6. **Tighter tax compliance.** GST collections from coaching more than doubled between 2019–20 and 2023–24 ([Factly](https://factly.in/data-gst-revenue-from-coaching-industry-more-than-doubled-between-2019-20-and-2023-24)), pushing centres toward digital receipts.

## Regulation checklist

| Area | Rule | Product response |
|---|---|---|
| DPDP Act 2023 + Rules 2025 | Notice, consent, purpose limitation, erasure, breach notification to the Board and affected people; data logs kept at least one year; 48-hour notice before deletion ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)) | Consent ledger, purpose tags per message type, deletion jobs, breach playbook |
| Children (under 18) | Verifiable parental consent via reliable ID/age check, e.g. DigiLocker; no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads, with narrow exemptions ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified-309928), [DPDPA Rule 10](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule10.html)) | Parent OTP + ID check at admission; no marketing templates to students; marketing only to opted-in parents |
| DPDP penalties | Up to ₹250 crore per breach of security safeguards; up to ₹200 crore for children's-data obligations (Schedule to the DPDP Act 2023, [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in)) | Treat as a top risk |
| Coaching guidelines 2024 | Registration with state; minimum age 16; tutors at least graduates; refund pro-rata within 10 days; no more than 5 hours of classes per day; website must list tutors, courses, fees, refund policy, enrolment numbers ([The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/education/new-guidelines-for-coaching-institutes-education-ministry)) | Compliance pack: age check, refund calculator, public institute page with required disclosures, class-hours check in timetable |
| Applicability of guidelines | Model guidelines; states must legislate. Reported to cover centres with more than 50 students and penalties of ₹25,000, then ₹1 lakh, then registration cancellation (verify against the official PDF at [education.gov.in](https://education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/document-reports/Guideliens_Coaching_Centres_en.pdf), which blocked automated access) | Track state notifications; open question in AGENTS.md |
| GST | Private coaching is taxable at 18% (SAC 9992/999293); exemption only for recognised qualifications ([GST Council flyer](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/GST_Education_Services.pdf), [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/academic-coaching-for-students-taxable-at-18gst-aarclarifies-scope-of-educational-institution-under-notification-read-order-1446273)); registration threshold ₹20 lakh turnover (₹10 lakh special-category states) | GST-aware receipts; GSTIN optional for small centres |
| WhatsApp policy | Opt-in required; template categories; marketing costs about 7.5× utility | Opt-in captured with consent; utility templates for alerts |

## Owner pain points (to validate in 20 discovery calls)

1. Fee defaulters and late payments (the most cited pain in the brief; quantify in pilots).
2. Parents complaining they did not know about absences.
3. Lead leakage in admission season.
4. Teacher attrition and payout disputes.
5. New compliance paperwork.
