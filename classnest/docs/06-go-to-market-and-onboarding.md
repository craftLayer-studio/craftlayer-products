# 06 Go-to-market and onboarding — ClassNest

## Launch timing

Launch the MVP by January so the first customers use the poster QR in the March–June admission season. Second peak: October–November (crash courses, new batches).

## Beachhead

One Tier 2 city with dense coaching streets (for example Indore's Bhanwarkuan, Jaipur's Gopalpura, Patna's Boring Road, Lucknow's Kapoorthala). One founder or sales rep can walk 30–40 institutes a week (estimate).

## Channels

| Channel | Tactic | Expected CAC | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Door-to-door in coaching hubs | 10-minute demo of the defaulter dashboard on the owner's own data (import 20 students live) | ₹3,000–5,000 | Acquisition |
| Parent-side virality | "Powered by ClassNest" on receipts, report cards and demo pages; link to a "Get this for your institute" page | Low | Referral |
| Owner referral | One month free for referrer and referee | ₹1,500 per deal | Referral |
| Partners | Printers who make admission flyers and banners (bundle the poster QR); CA firms that file coaching GST; stationery and book distributors | 15% of first-year revenue | Acquisition |
| Local associations | Coaching federation chapters; talk on "new coaching rules + DPDP: what to do" | Low | Acquisition |
| Search and YouTube | Hindi videos: "Fee defaulters kaise kam karein", "Coaching registration rules 2024" | ₹2,000–4,000 | Acquisition |
| Meta ads (Feb–Apr) | Lead form to book a demo; target owners via interest and location | ₹4,000–7,000 | Acquisition |

## Getting the first 10 paying customers

1. **Weeks 1–2: 20 discovery visits.** Ask about last month's fee shortfall in rupees. Collect five letters of intent.
2. **Weeks 3–8: 5 design partners.** Free for 3 months in exchange for weekly feedback and a video testimonial. Choose a mix: 2 tuition centres (Class 9–12), 1 competitive-exam centre, 1 music academy, 1 spoken-English or computer centre.
3. **Week 8: convert partners.** Offer the annual Growth plan at ₹9,999 (founding price) if they continue. Target 4 of 5.
4. **Weeks 9–12: 6 more.** Use partner testimonials ("collected ₹X more in March") in the same street. Run a 1-hour workshop in a partner's classroom for 15 nearby owners.
5. **Offer for the first 50:** founding price locked for 2 years, free poster kit, setup done for them.

## Sales script (10 minutes)

1. **Open:** "How much fee is pending from last month, roughly?" Wait for the number.
2. **Pain:** "How much time does your staff spend calling parents about fees and absences?"
3. **Demo (on their data):** Import 20 students from their register photo or Excel. Send a real fee reminder with a UPI link to the owner's phone. Mark one student absent and show the parent alert.
4. **Admission hook:** "Print this QR on your April flyers. Every scan becomes a lead with a demo booking."
5. **Price:** "₹1,499 a month on the yearly plan. If it recovers one fee a month, it pays for itself."
6. **Close:** "Start the 14-day trial today. We set it up for you before the evening batch."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Teachmint is free." | "Free apps help you teach online. ClassNest gets your fees in and tells parents about absences. Try both for 14 days and compare collections." |
| "We already use Classplus." | "Keep Classplus for videos if you like. ClassNest costs less than Classplus and runs your front desk: attendance, fees, admissions. Many centres use both." |
| "Parents will not like reminders." | "Reminders are polite, in Hindi or English, and parents can pay in two taps. Parents get a receipt every time, which they like." |
| "My staff will not use it." | "Attendance is one tap per batch. We train your staff in 20 minutes on-site." |
| "Too expensive." | "Starter is ₹799 a month on the yearly plan. Show me one month where you did not have a ₹800 pending fee." |
| "Data privacy worries." | "Parents give consent at admission. No ads, no selling data. We follow the DPDP Act and delete data when you ask." |
| "Admissions season is over." | "Use the pause plan at ₹199 and your enquiry page stays live for next season." |

## Onboarding checklist (target: live in 1 day, activated in 7 days)

| Step | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Create institute, add GSTIN and state registration number (if any) | Owner with CSM | Profile complete |
| 2. Import students, guardians, batches from Excel or register photo | CSM | 100% of active students loaded |
| 3. Send consent requests to guardians (OTP + ID check for under-18s) | System | 80% consents in 7 days |
| 4. Set fee plans and import pending dues | Owner | Defaulter dashboard shows real total |
| 5. Connect Razorpay account (or use ClassNest's sub-merchant onboarding) | Owner | First UPI test payment received |
| 6. Print and place entrance QR standee | CSM | Photo of QR at door |
| 7. Add teachers; install staff app on their phones | CSM | Each teacher marks one batch |
| 8. Approve WhatsApp templates in Hindi/English | System | Templates live |
| 9. First reminder run for upcoming dues | System | Reminders sent |
| 10. Create poster QR campaign for the next admission drive | Owner | Poster file downloaded |
| 11. Day-7 review call: show attendance %, fees collected via ClassNest | CSM | Owner confirms value |

Activation definition: attendance marked on 5 of the first 7 days and at least one UPI fee collected.

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| No attendance for 3 days | WhatsApp nudge to owner; CSM call |
| Fee volume via ClassNest falling | Check if staff reverted to cash; retrain |
| Before exam season ends (Feb–Mar) | Offer pause plan instead of cancellation |
| Annual renewal in 30 days | Send "your year in numbers": fees collected, absences caught, leads converted |
| High NPS (9–10) | Ask for referral and Google review |

## Metrics to watch weekly

Trials started, activation rate, trial-to-paid, fee GMV through ClassNest, WhatsApp cost per account, churn and pauses, referrals.
