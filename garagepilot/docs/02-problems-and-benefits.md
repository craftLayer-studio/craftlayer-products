# 02 Problems and benefits — GaragePilot

## Owner

| Problem | GaragePilot solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customers leave after one visit; no system to call them back | Service-due engine by km and date; reminders with booking link | Predictable repeat revenue | Retention |
| Disputes over extra work and damage at delivery | Check-in photos and video; line-item approvals on WhatsApp | Fewer arguments, fewer write-offs | Revenue |
| Low average bill per car; advisors do not upsell | Photos of worn parts; declined-work follow-ups | Higher repair order value | Revenue (ARPU) |
| Paper job cards, lost history | Vehicle history searchable by number plate | Faster diagnosis, better advice | Activation |
| Parts stock leaks and unknown margins | Inventory with purchase entries and part-wise margin | Less pilferage, better pricing | Revenue |
| GST invoices typed by hand; parts rates changed in September 2025 | GST invoice with HSN/SAC and correct rates | Clean books, fewer errors | Revenue |
| Insurance payments stuck for weeks | Claim tracker with days outstanding per insurer | Faster follow-up, better cash flow | Revenue |
| Wash plans in a notebook | Subscriptions with QR redemption and AutoPay | Recurring revenue | Revenue, retention |
| Cannot see which mechanic is productive | Task tracking and job counts per mechanic | Fair incentives | Retention |

## Staff (service advisor, mechanic)

| Problem | GaragePilot solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3–4 calls per car for approvals (estimate) | WhatsApp approval link | Calls cut, bays move faster | Activation |
| Vague complaints cause rework | Voice notes, photos, checklist | Less rework | Retention |
| Customers call asking "is my car ready?" | Live status link | Fewer calls | Retention |
| Incentive disputes | Jobs per mechanic recorded | Transparent payouts | Retention |

## Vehicle owner (end customer)

| Problem | GaragePilot solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distrust of local garages | Photos of the problem before work, line-item approval | Confidence to keep using the garage | Retention |
| No idea when the car will be ready | Live status page | Plans the day | Retention |
| Forgets service intervals | Reminder by km and date | Car stays healthy; avoids costly breakdowns | Retention |
| Paper bills lost; needs GST invoice for company reimbursement | Digital GST invoice on WhatsApp | Easy reimbursement and resale history | Revenue |
| Insurance claims confusing | Claim status updates | Less stress | Retention |

## Fleet customer

| Problem | GaragePilot solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| No per-vehicle maintenance record | Vehicle history and monthly statement | Lower downtime, audit trail | Revenue |

## Why the owner pays (one sentence)

If reminders bring back two extra cars a month at an average bill of ₹3,000–5,000 (estimate), the Workshop plan (₹1,999) pays for itself several times over.
