# 03 Pros, cons, risks — GaragePilot

## Pros

| Pro | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Large post-warranty shift to independent garages | Stellantis India estimates about 65% of owners move to local garages after warranty ([S&P Global Mobility](https://aftermarketinsight.spglobal.com/news/4320/eurorepar-eyes-indias-independent-aftermarket-interview-with-stellantis-india-ceo-md), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Fast-growing segment | Multi-brand car service estimated at USD 8.81 billion in 2025, 10.7% CAGR ([Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-multi-brand-car-service-market.md), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Repeat revenue is measurable | Reminders produce bookings the owner can count, which proves ROI |
| Higher ticket size than most CraftLayer verticals | Garages bill lakhs per month, so ₹2,000/month is easy to justify |
| Daily use | Every vehicle needs a job card |
| Shared core | QR, payments, CRM, WhatsApp, reviews reused |

## Cons

| Con | Impact |
|---|---|
| Many cheap or desktop billing tools (Vyapar, Busy, RAMP at ₹9,870/yr) | Owners compare on price; must show reminders and approvals ROI |
| Mechanics have low smartphone and typing comfort | Voice input, photos, regional languages required |
| Parts data is messy (no clean catalogue for every make/model) | Inventory setup takes time |
| Garages are busy and noisy; onboarding needs a visit | Higher CAC than pure self-serve |
| Insurance claim workflows differ by insurer | Tracker stays manual at first |

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owners keep paper job cards and use the app only for invoices | High | High | Make the job card faster than paper (under 2 minutes); photos as the hook; activation SLA |
| WhatsApp reminders classified as marketing (₹0.8631 per message) | Medium | Medium | Bundle 300 reminders; credit packs; test utility classification; SMS fallback |
| Competitors (GaragePlug, Autorox) add WhatsApp approvals and cut prices | Medium | Medium | Speed of setup, price, service-due engine quality, regional language |
| Aggregators (GoMechanic-style) capture customers and pressure garages | Medium | Low | Position as the garage's own brand and CRM; aggregator jobs can be tagged as a source |
| EV shift reduces service frequency | Low (next 3 years) | Medium | EVs were about 8% of 2025 registrations ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/indias-ev-sales-touch-23-million-in-2025-as-market-share-rises-to-8-per-cent)); add EV checklists, tyres, brakes, detailing, battery health |
| Data protection: customer phone numbers and vehicle data | Medium | Medium | DPDP-compliant notice and consent at check-in; opt-out link on every reminder |
| Photo/video storage cost grows | Medium | Low | Compress on device; 12-month retention on Bay plan; archive tiers |
| Seasonal dips (monsoon, festivals) | Low | Low | Monsoon check-up campaigns; festive detailing offers |

## Kill criteria

Review at month 6 after launch. Stop or pivot if two or more hold:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Paying garages | Fewer than 40 |
| Trial-to-paid | Below 15% |
| Garages creating at least 50% of job cards in GaragePilot after 60 days | Below 50% |
| Monthly logo churn | Above 5% |
| CAC payback | Over 9 months |
| Reminder-to-booking conversion | Below 8% (the core value is not landing) |

Pivot options: narrow to car wash and detailing subscriptions only (simpler, recurring), or sell the service-due reminder engine as an add-on to existing billing tools (Vyapar/Busy users).
