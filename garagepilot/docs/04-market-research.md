## Trends 2024–2026

1. **GST cut on auto parts.** All auto parts moved to a uniform 18% GST (from a mix of 18% and 28%), and small cars and two-wheelers up to 350cc to 18%, effective 22 September 2025 ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=149276), [Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/gst-cut-on-autos-farm-machinery-to-boost-demand-128419)). Organised garages with proper invoices become more price-competitive; invoices must use the new rates.
2. **Record vehicle sales.** Passenger and two-wheeler sales hit records in 2025, growing the out-of-warranty parc 3–5 years later.
3. **Cashless insurance networks expanding.** HDFC ERGO reports 12,277 active network garages as of February 2025 ([HDFC ERGO](https://www.hdfcergo.com/campaigns/car-insurance-4)); Navi General partnered with GoMechanic and ReadyAssist for cashless claims ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/navi-general-insurance-expands-cashless-motor-claims-network-through-new-partnerships-132547)). Claim tracking becomes a real need for independents.
4. **Aggregator consolidation.** GoMechanic restructured after 2023 and reported ₹210 crore revenue in FY2023-24 ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/gomechanic-eyes-over-3-fold-biz-growth-to-rs-700-crore-by-2027-before-launching-ipo)); garages want their own customer relationships.
5. **Subscriptions in car wash.** Market reports list monthly memberships and multi-wash passes as a business model ([Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-car-wash-market.md)); car detailing franchising is growing ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/impact-feature/story/why-car-detailing-is-indias-next-high-roi-franchise-boom-508209-2025-12-26)).
6. **WhatsApp per-message pricing.** Since 1 July 2025; India marketing ₹0.8631, utility about ₹0.115 per message ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)).
7. **EV growth.** About 8% of 2025 registrations; EVs need fewer oil services but still need tyres, brakes, suspension, AC, detailing and body work.

## Regulation checklist

| Area | Rule | Product response |
|---|---|---|
| DPDP Act 2023 + Rules 2025 | Notice and consent for phone numbers; purpose limitation; erasure on request; breach notification; data logs kept at least one year; most obligations apply 18 months after November 2025 notification ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)) | Consent checkbox and notice at check-in; separate opt-in for promotional messages; opt-out on every reminder |
| GST | Parts at 18% (uniform since 22 Sep 2025); labour/repair service at 18% (verify per job type); e-invoicing only above the turnover threshold | HSN/SAC on lines; CGST/SGST/IGST split; GSTR-1 export (P2) |
| Motor insurance | Cashless repair only at insurer network garages; surveyor approves estimate; claims at non-network garages become reimbursement ([Bajaj General](https://www.bajajgeneralinsurance.com/motor-insurance/cashless-claim-settlement-in-car-insurance.html)) | Claim tracker with stages and documents |
| WhatsApp policy | Opt-in; template categories; marketing costs about 7.5× utility | Utility for status, estimates, invoices; reminders budgeted as marketing |
| Consumer protection | Customers can dispute unauthorised work | Line-item approval log with timestamp is evidence |

## Owner pain points (to validate in 20 discovery calls)

1. Customers do not come back; no way to remind them.
2. Estimate disputes and "you changed parts I did not ask for".
3. Approvals delayed because customers do not answer calls.
4. Parts stock leakage and unknown margins.
5. Insurance payments delayed.
