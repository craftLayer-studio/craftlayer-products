# 06 Go-to-market and onboarding — GaragePilot

## Beachhead

One city with dense garage clusters (for example Pune's Bhosari and Kondhwa, Ahmedabad's SG Highway belt, Jaipur's Transport Nagar, Lucknow's Kapoorthala, Chandigarh's Industrial Area). Independent garages cluster on the same roads, so one rep can visit 30–40 a week (estimate).

## Getting the first 10 paying customers

1. **Weeks 1–2: 20 visits.** Ask: "How many customers from last year came back this year?" and "How many estimate disputes last month?" Note the numbers.
2. **Weeks 3–8: 5 design partners.** 3 car garages, 1 two-wheeler centre, 1 car wash/detailing studio. Free for 3 months, weekly feedback, video testimonial.
3. **Back-fill reminders on day 1.** Import the garage's old billing data (Vyapar/Excel) and send service-due reminders to past customers in week one. The owner sees bookings within 14 days: the fastest proof of value.
4. **Week 8: convert.** Founding offer: Workshop annual at ₹14,999 (25% off) locked for 2 years. Target 4 of 5.
5. **Weeks 9–12: 6 more** from the same roads, using "Sandeep's garage got 23 bookings from reminders in March" style proof. Ask the local parts dealer to host a 1-hour evening session for 15 garage owners.

## Sales script (10 minutes)

1. **Open:** "How many of last year's customers came back for their next service?"
2. **Pain:** "When you call a customer to approve extra work, how often do they not pick up?"
3. **Demo:** Pick a car in the bay. Take 4 photos, build a 3-line estimate, send to the owner's WhatsApp. Owner approves 2 lines, declines 1 on his own phone.
4. **Money moment:** Show the service-due list: "These 40 customers are due this month. We remind them automatically."
5. **Price:** "₹1,999 a month on the yearly plan, unlimited mechanics. Two extra cars a month and it's paid."
6. **Close:** "We import your last year's bills today and start reminders tomorrow."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I already use Vyapar/Busy for billing." | "Keep it for accounts if you want. GaragePilot brings customers back and gets approvals. We also make GST invoices, so many garages drop the second tool." |
| "My mechanics cannot use apps." | "They tap a status button and take photos. Advisors use voice in Hindi. We train them in your garage." |
| "Customers do not like messages." | "These are their own car's updates and due dates. Every message has an opt-out. Customers prefer it to calls." |
| "GaragePlug/Autorox showed me software." | "Ask them for a price on paper. We are ₹1,999 a month with no per-user charges, live today." |
| "Too expensive." | "Bay is ₹999 a month. One extra oil service covers it." |
| "I'll lose my data if I stop." | "Export all job cards and customers to Excel any time." |

## Onboarding checklist (target: live same day, activated in 7 days)

| Step | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Create garage, GSTIN, invoice series, labour rates | Owner with CSM | First test invoice correct |
| 2. Import past customers, vehicles and bills (Vyapar/Excel/register photo) | CSM | Customer list loaded; service-due list generated |
| 3. Set service intervals by vehicle type (car petrol/diesel, bike, EV) | CSM | Default rules confirmed |
| 4. Add parts list and opening stock (top 100 fast-moving parts first) | Owner/advisor | Stock visible |
| 5. Add staff; install staff app on advisor and mechanic phones | CSM | Each mechanic updates one job |
| 6. Approve WhatsApp templates (check-in, estimate, ready, invoice, reminder) | System | Templates live |
| 7. Place QR stickers on job card book and counter standee | CSM | Photo uploaded |
| 8. Connect Razorpay / UPI | Owner | First UPI payment received |
| 9. Send first batch of service-due reminders to past customers | System | Reminders sent |
| 10. Connect Google review link | CSM | Review request live |
| 11. Day-7 call: job cards created, approvals via WhatsApp, bookings from reminders | CSM | Owner confirms value |

Activation definition: 20 job cards with photos and at least one WhatsApp estimate approval in the first 7 days.

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Job cards per day falling below 50% of baseline | Call owner; check if advisors went back to paper |
| Reminder credits exhausted | Offer credit pack; show bookings generated |
| Monsoon (June–September) | Pre-built monsoon check-up campaign |
| Festive season (Oct–Nov) | Detailing and wash-pass campaigns |
| Renewal in 30 days | "Your year": repeat customers, approved estimate value, reminder bookings |
| NPS 9–10 | Ask for referral and a review; invite to parts-dealer session as speaker |

## Metrics to watch weekly

Trials started, activation rate, trial-to-paid, job cards per garage per day, WhatsApp approval rate, reminder-to-booking conversion, message cost per account, churn.
