# NestKeep — problems and benefits

Each row maps a problem to the NestKeep feature and a measurable benefit. Unsourced numbers are labelled **(estimate)** with reasoning. Sources accessed 2026-11-24.

## Owner

| Problem | Evidence | NestKeep solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent arrives late; owner chases on WhatsApp and calls | A PG software vendor estimates leakage across rent, deposits, utilities, vacancies and maintenance at 15–20% of revenue (vendor estimate, not a survey) ([RentOk blog](https://rentok.com/blogs/pg-owner-app/5-ways-pg-owners-lose-money-every-month-and-how-to-fix-it)) | UPI Autopay, payment links, reminders at −3/0/+3/+7 days, automatic late fee | Rent collected by due date + 5 rises from about 70% to 90%+ **(estimate: reminders plus Autopay remove forgetfulness, the main cause)** |
| Food cooked for residents who do not eat | Vendor guide notes kitchens waste food when attendance is lower than planned ([RentOk](https://rentok.com/blogs/market-trends/how-to-manage-food-operations-in-pg)) | Meal opt-out by QR with cut-off; headcount to kitchen | 50-resident PG, 2 meals a day, ₹45 raw cost per meal = ₹1,35,000 per month. If 15% is wasted and opt-out halves it, saves about ₹10,000 per month **(estimate)** |
| Electricity bills rise with ACs and heaters; flat charge under-recovers | Owner complaint, no survey **(estimate)** | Sub-meter readings per room, split by occupied bed-days, added to invoice | Recover actual usage; fewer disputes because residents see readings and photos |
| Empty beds between exits and new move-ins | Owners rely on brokers and listings **(estimate)** | Notice tracking forecasts vacancy; lead page and inbox start filling 30 days early | Fill a ₹9,000 bed 10 days sooner = ₹3,000 per vacancy **(estimate)** |
| Verification and licence paperwork | Bengaluru police directive makes tenant verification mandatory ([NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/how-to-do-tenant-verification-in-bangalore/)); reporters found 5 of 10 Bengaluru PGs did not ask for ID ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-pg-security-gaps-persist-despite-bbmp-guidelines-3665367)); 3,597 of 5,480 audited PGs lacked licences ([OneIndia](https://www.oneindia.com/bengaluru/bengaluru-pg-audit-finds-3-597-facilities-operating-without-licences-east-tops-list-8243241.html)) | KYC vault, police-verification packet, status tracker, register export | Inspection-ready in minutes; lower risk of sealing or fines |
| Complaints get lost; bad reviews on Google and listing sites | Tenant-side reports of poor food and maintenance ([The Wire](https://thewire.in/livewire/mice-infested-kitchens-leaking-ceilings-and-poor-food-the-cost-of-studying-in-delhi)) | QR tickets with SLA and resident rating | Faster fixes, better reviews, lower churn |
| Deposit disputes at exit | About 150 of 400 surveyed Delhi students said previous landlords had not returned deposits ([Newslaundry](https://newslaundry.com/amp/story/2024%2F08%2F10%2Fhigh-rent-narrow-rooms-is-landlord-broker-nexus-behind-delhi-coaching-hub-crisis)) | Notice tracker, exit checklist, itemised settlement statement | Fewer disputes; trust signal for new residents |
| No view of profit across buildings | Excel only | Multi-property dashboard (P2) | Decide where to expand or cut |

## Staff (warden, resident manager, cook, cleaner)

| Problem | NestKeep solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Residents message at all hours | Tickets in a queue with category and room | Fewer interruptions, clear priorities |
| Kitchen guesses quantities | Meal headcount after cut-off | Less waste, fewer "food ran out" complaints |
| Manual visitor book | QR visitor log with host approval | Accurate record, less gate argument |
| Collecting cash rent | Autopay and links; cash entries logged | Less cash handling, less suspicion |

## Resident

| Problem | NestKeep solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Unclear bills, surprise charges | Itemised monthly invoice with meter photo | Transparency |
| Complaints ignored | QR ticket with status and time | Faster fixes |
| Paying for meals not eaten | Opt-out (owner may offer credit per policy) | Fairness, possible rent credit |
| Deposit not returned | Settlement statement and UPI refund | Trust |
| Remembering due date | Autopay or reminder with pay link | No late fee |
| ID copies lying around | Masked Aadhaar, encrypted storage, deletion after exit period | Privacy |

## Parent (for student residents)

| Problem | NestKeep solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Worry about safety | Verified residents, visitor log | Confidence in the PG |
| No proof of payment | Receipts to parent number (with resident consent) | Clear records |

## Benefit summary for the sales pitch

| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| "Rent on time without a single call" | Autopay plus reminders plus late fee |
| "Pays for itself from food savings alone" | 50 beds on Pro = ₹1,750 per month vs about ₹10,000 per month food saving **(estimate)** |
| "Inspection-ready records" | KYC vault, verification tracker, visitor log |
| "Fill beds before they empty" | Notice forecast plus lead page |
