## Trends 2024–2026

1. **Formalisation pressure**: Bengaluru licence audits and sealing backed by the High Court ([OneIndia](https://www.oneindia.com/bengaluru/over-3-500-bengaluru-pgs-face-sealing-as-karnataka-high-court-backs-gba-crackdown-8243497.html)); Delhi draft PG law with licences, police verification and a public registry ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/new-delhi/delhi-plans-pg-regulation-bill-with-licences-police-clearance-and-public-registry-1119952)).
2. **Demand shift in Bengaluru**: closures in 2025 blamed on new rules, tech layoffs and rising costs ([News Karnataka](https://newskarnataka.com/bengaluru/bengaluru-pgs-in-crisis-new-rules-layoffs-and-soaring-costs-force-shutdowns/02062025/)). Owners who survive need tighter margins and fuller beds.
3. **Organised co-living tripling by 2030** (Colliers): institutional operators raise the bar on resident experience; owner-run PGs must match it.
4. **UPI Autopay maturity**: RBI's 2026 e-mandate framework allows recurring debits up to ₹15,000 without additional authentication per transaction ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-caps-recurring-payments-at-rs15000-without-otp-under-new-e-mandate-framework-526759-2026-04-21), [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Notification.aspx?Id=13374)). Most PG beds sit under this cap **(estimate)**.
5. **Tenancy law reform**: Model Tenancy Act caps residential deposits at 2 months and pushes registered agreements; adopted by Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Assam; Karnataka reported to mirror deposit caps from January 2026 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/real-estate/story/model-tenancy-act-how-new-security-deposits-rent-agreement-entry-exit-rules-will-benefit-tenants-landlords-504839-2025-12-03), [RentOk](https://rentok.com/blogs/legal/navigating-the-new-model-tenancy-act-a-state-by-state-guide-for-2026)).
6. **WhatsApp per-message pricing**: utility messages (reminders, receipts) about ₹0.115 each in India; marketing about ₹0.86 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)). Rent reminders are cheap utility traffic.

## Owner pain points (ranked)

1. Late and missed rent; time spent chasing (vendor evidence, owner interviews needed).
2. Vacancy between exits and new residents **(estimate)**.
3. Food cost and waste ([RentOk](https://rentok.com/blogs/market-trends/how-to-manage-food-operations-in-pg)).
4. Compliance: verification, licence, inspections (sourced above).
5. Complaints and online reputation (tenant-side reports above).
