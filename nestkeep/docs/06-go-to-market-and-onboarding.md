# NestKeep — go-to-market and onboarding

## Launch strategy in one paragraph

Start in one dense PG cluster in one city (for example Marathahalli–Bellandur–HSR in Bengaluru, or Madhapur–Gachibowli in Hyderabad). These areas have hundreds of PGs within walking distance **(estimate)**, owners meet at local associations, and city crackdowns make records urgent. Win 5 free pilots, publish their rent-on-time and food-saving numbers, then sell door-to-door and through PG owners' associations. Add a second city (Pune or a coaching hub such as Kota) after month 6.

## First 10 paying customers: step-by-step

| Step | Action | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build a list of 200 PGs in the cluster from Google Maps, NoBroker, Housing and street walk (20+ beds) | Prospect sheet with owner phone, beds, food yes/no | 3 days |
| 2 | Visit 40; run the 10-minute demo; ask for last month's rent sheet | 12–15 interested owners | Week 1–2 |
| 3 | Offer 5 owners a free 45-day pilot (one full rent cycle plus buffer): we import residents, place QRs, set reminders | 5 live pilots | Week 2–3 |
| 4 | Measure baseline: % rent paid by day 5, meals cooked vs eaten for 3 days, open complaints | Baseline numbers | Week 3 |
| 5 | Run the rent cycle; visit day 2 and day 8 after due date; send owner a weekly WhatsApp summary | Before-after numbers | Week 4–7 |
| 6 | Show results and ask for annual Pro; founding offer ₹25 per bed on Pro for the first 20 customers, locked 12 months | 3–4 paying | Week 7 |
| 7 | Ask each paying owner for 2 introductions (one free month per paid referral) | 6–8 warm leads | Week 8 |
| 8 | Speak at the local PG owners' association meeting with a pilot owner | 10–20 leads | Week 8–9 |
| 9 | Close to 10 paying | 10 paying properties, about 600 beds | Week 10 |

## Demo script (10 minutes)

1. **Hook (1 minute):** "How many residents paid late last month, and how many days did you spend collecting?" Write it down.
2. **Rent (3 minutes):** Show a sample resident's ledger, the reminder WhatsApp at −3 days, the pay link, the Autopay setup, the auto receipt, and late fee added on day 4.
3. **Bed board (1 minute):** "You have 3 people on notice; 3 beds empty on the 1st unless you act now." Show the lead page.
4. **QR (3 minutes):** Owner scans a sample room QR: raise a complaint, opt out of dinner, add a visitor. Show the kitchen headcount screen.
5. **Compliance (1 minute):** Show a resident's KYC record and the police-verification packet.
6. **Close (1 minute):** "We will load all your residents and put QRs in every room in 2 days. First rent cycle free. If your on-time rent does not improve, we take it all down."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "My residents pay by GPay already." | "Keep GPay. NestKeep sends the reminder, the link and the receipt, and marks it paid. You stop checking screenshots." |
| "RentOk is free." | "Use what works. NestKeep adds food opt-out, police-verification records, visitor log and a lead page, and we set it all up in 2 days. Food savings alone cover the fee." |
| "Residents will not like late fees." | "The fee is your rule, in the agreement, and you can waive it with one tap. Most residents just pay on time once reminded." |
| "Per bed is expensive for empty beds." | "₹35 is under 0.5% of a ₹9,000 rent. One bed filled a week earlier pays for the whole month." |
| "My manager is not tech-savvy." | "The manager only taps 'Done' on tickets and enters meter readings once a month with a photo. We train them in their language." |
| "Aadhaar data is risky." | "We store only masked Aadhaar, encrypted, deleted after exit. That is safer than photocopies in a cupboard, and it is what the new data law expects." |
| "Students leave in summer." | "Billing has a monthly minimum, and annual plans are 2 months free. Your lead page fills beds for the new batch." |

## Onboarding checklist (target: live in 48 hours)

| # | Task | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create account; add properties, floors, rooms, beds, sharing types and rents | NestKeep ops | Bed board matches owner's register |
| 2 | Import residents (Excel, photo of notebook, or WhatsApp list) with rent, deposit, joining date | Ops | All occupied beds have a tenancy |
| 3 | Set rent due day, reminder schedule, late-fee rule, notice days | Owner + ops | Rules saved and shown in agreement template |
| 4 | Connect owner's payment account (Razorpay / Cashfree, owner as merchant) | Owner | Test ₹1 payment reaches owner's bank |
| 5 | Approve WhatsApp utility templates (reminder, receipt, ticket update) | Ops | Templates approved |
| 6 | Send resident invite links: profile, KYC upload, consent, Autopay option | Ops | 80% residents opened link |
| 7 | Print and place QRs: each room, kitchen, common area, gate (visitor log) | Field rep | Photo of every placement |
| 8 | Add staff: manager, cook, cleaner, maintenance; map ticket categories | Owner | Test ticket closed by each |
| 9 | Pro: set weekly menu and opt-out cut-off times | Owner + cook | Kitchen sees tomorrow's headcount |
| 10 | Pro: record meter IDs and opening readings per room | Manager | All meters have opening reading |
| 11 | Pro: select city for police-verification packet; mark existing verification status | Owner | Every resident has a status |
| 12 | Pro: publish vacancy lead page; add link to Google Business Profile and listings | Ops | Test lead received |
| 13 | 20-minute staff training in local language | Field rep | Staff complete 3 test actions |
| 14 | Day 3 call, first rent-cycle review on due date + 5, day 30 review meeting | Customer success | Logged in CRM |

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Under 50% residents opened invite after 7 days | Field rep visits; manager collects at dinner time |
| Rent on time below 80% after first cycle | Review reminder timing; push Autopay with owner-funded ₹100 discount |
| Owner not logged in for 7 days | Weekly WhatsApp digest with one highlighted win |
| Tickets open over 48 hours | Alert owner; review staff assignment |
| Opt-out usage under 30% of residents | Put menu QR on dining table; announce cut-off in group |
| Vacancy above 10% | Offer vacancy boost; share lead page link |
| Month 3 on monthly plan | Offer annual with 2 months free |
| Before May–June student exits | Campaign: "Fill your beds for the new batch" |
