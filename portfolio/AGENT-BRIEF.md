# Brief for research/writing agents (internal)

Portfolio: CraftLayer builds vertical SaaS for offline small businesses in India. Common wedge: a QR code placed in the business (table, mirror, gym wall, counter, room) that lets walk-in customers self-serve (order, book, pay, check in, review, play a game) while the owner captures the customer's phone number with consent and builds a repeat-customer CRM with WhatsApp marketing.

Shared core platform (see `core/README.md`) provides: multi-tenant accounts, QR engine, public storefront/menu/catalog pages, ordering & booking engine, payments (Razorpay/UPI), customer CRM, WhatsApp/SMS messaging, loyalty & rewards, mini-games, reviews & Google review funnel, staff app, analytics dashboard, onboarding wizard.
