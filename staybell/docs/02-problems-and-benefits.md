# StayBell — problems and benefits

Each row maps a real problem to the StayBell feature that solves it and the measurable benefit. Numbers without a source are labelled **(estimate)** with reasoning.

## Owner

| Problem | Evidence | StayBell solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTA commission eats margin | Hoteliers have reported MakeMyTrip-Goibibo commissions of 20–40%; MMT-Go said it charges most partners under 25% ([Zee Business](https://www.zeebiz.com/india/news-ahmedabad-hoteliers-boycott-makemytrip-goibibo-on-heavy-commission-discounts-74290), accessed 2026-11-24). Booking.com averages about 15% with a 10–25% range ([Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com/online-travel-agencies/commissions/), accessed 2026-11-24) | Guest CRM, direct booking site with 0% commission, WhatsApp offers to past guests | A 15-room property at ₹3,000 ADR and 60% occupancy sells 270 room nights a month. Moving 27 of them (10%) from an 18% OTA to direct saves about ₹14,600 a month **(estimate)** |
| Margin squeeze after GST change | Rooms up to ₹7,500 moved to 5% GST without input tax credit from 22 September 2025 ([VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/09/22/gst-rate-cut-to-5-for-budget-hotels-under-%e2%82%b97500-restricts-input-tax-credit/), accessed 2026-11-24) | Same as above, plus in-stay upsell revenue | Lost ITC can be offset by commission savings and upsells |
| Owner does not own the guest | OTAs mask or limit guest contact details (common owner complaint, estimate) | Digital check-in captures phone and consent for every guest, including OTA guests | Contactable guest list grows every night |
| Room-service orders by phone are missed or not billed | No sourced data; owner interviews needed **(estimate)** | Room QR menu with order ticket and room-bill posting | F&B revenue per occupied room up 10–20% **(estimate: easier ordering plus photos and upsell prompts)** |
| Paper guest register and ID photocopies | Police norms require a guest register; foreign guests need Form C within 24 hours ([india.gov.in](https://www.india.gov.in/online-form-c-bureau-immigration), accessed 2026-11-24) | Digital register, encrypted ID vault, Form C data pack with reminders | Fewer missed filings, faster police inspections, DPDP-ready records |
| Bad reviews appear before the owner can act | Review score drives OTA ranking (industry consensus, estimate) | Complaint button in room, 15-minute escalation, private feedback for low ratings | More issues fixed in-stay; more 5-star public reviews |
| No idea which guests are repeat or high-value | Excel or paper only | CRM with stay history, spend, source | Target the right guests for slow dates |

## Staff (front desk, caretaker, kitchen, housekeeping)

| Problem | StayBell solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Constant calls to the desk and owner's mobile | Requests arrive as tickets in the staff app with room number | Fewer interruptions; clear queue |
| Orders misheard on phone | Written order with item, quantity, notes | Fewer remakes and disputes |
| Blame for slow service without proof | Timestamps for request created and resolved | Fair performance tracking |
| Re-typing guest details for register and Form C | Guest fills it once on their phone | 5–10 minutes saved per check-in **(estimate: typical paper form plus photocopy time)** |

## Guest (domestic and foreign)

| Problem | StayBell solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting at reception for forms and ID photocopy | Pre-arrival or QR check-in on own phone | Faster arrival |
| Calling reception repeatedly for towels, water, Wi-Fi | Room QR page with one-tap requests and Wi-Fi details | Less friction, no app install |
| No menu or prices in room | Digital menu with photos and prices | Clear choice, pay by UPI |
| Unsure about local trips and taxis | Curated experiences from the property | Trusted local options |
| Pays more on OTA than needed | Direct booking offer for next stay | Lower price or extra perks direct |
| Privacy worry about ID copies | Clear notice, masked Aadhaar, deletion after retention period | Trust |

## Benefit summary for the sales pitch

| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|

| "Foreign guest paperwork ready in 1 minute" | Form C fields captured from guest upload; owner copies into portal |
