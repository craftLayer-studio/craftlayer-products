# StayBell — pros, cons, risks and kill criteria

## Pros

| Pro | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Visible value on day 1 | QR standees in rooms, guests scan, owner sees requests the same night |
| Clear money story | OTA commission is the owner's most resented cost; savings are easy to calculate |
| Low price vs PMS | ₹999–₹4,999 per month sits beside, not against, PMS tools priced from about ₹2,250 per month (eZee, per [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/ezee-absolute), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Reuses CraftLayer core | QR, ordering, CRM, WhatsApp, reviews already built for DineLoop; StayBell is mostly configuration plus hotel modules |
| Compliance tailwind | DPDP Rules 2025 and Form C make a structured digital register valuable |
| Data network effect inside each property | Every stay adds to the owner's CRM; switching away loses history |

## Cons

| Con | Impact |
|---|---|
| Not a PMS or channel manager | Some owners want one tool; we must integrate or lose deals to eZee/Djubo bundles |
| Highly seasonal customers | Hill and beach properties have dead months; churn risk in off-season |
| Fragmented, low-tech owners | Field sales needed; slow onboarding in Tier 3 |
| Direct booking needs traffic | A booking site without marketing gets few bookings; benefit depends on CRM campaigns |
| OTAs can retaliate | Rate parity clauses and ranking penalties make owners nervous about pushing direct |

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMS vendors add a room QR module | High | Medium | Move fast on CRM and direct booking; integrate with them rather than compete; price below their add-ons |
| Owners do not run campaigns, so no direct bookings appear | High | High | Done-for-you monthly campaign included in Direct plan; pre-written templates; track direct share in dashboard |
| Guests do not scan QR | Medium | High | Standee at bedside and desk, "Wi-Fi password inside" hook, staff script at check-in |
| ID data breach | Low | Very high | Encrypted storage, masked Aadhaar, role-based access, auto-deletion, audit log; DPDP breach-notice process |
| WhatsApp template rejections or number bans | Medium | Medium | Utility templates for service messages; marketing only to opted-in guests; quality monitoring |
| WhatsApp cost increases | Medium | Low | Pass-through billing at Meta cost plus margin; marketing rate in India is about ₹0.86 per message ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Form C process change or new API | Low | Medium | Data pack format is configurable; monitor Bureau of Immigration notices |
| Seasonal churn | High | Medium | Annual plans with 2 months free; "pause plan" at ₹299 per month in off-season keeps data |
| Rate parity clause disputes | Medium | Medium | Offer perks (breakfast, late checkout) instead of lower price on direct site |

## Kill criteria

Review at the end of each stage. If any condition holds, stop or pivot.

| Checkpoint | Kill or pivot if |
|---|---|
| Week 8 (pilot end) | Fewer than 3 of 5 pilot properties agree to pay at least ₹999 per month |
| Week 8 | Fewer than 40% of rooms show any QR scan in the pilot week |
| Month 4 | Fewer than 15 paying properties, or blended CAC above ₹15,000 |
| Month 6 | Monthly logo churn above 6% for two consecutive months (excluding paused plans) |
| Month 6 | Direct-plan customers show no increase in direct room nights (median under +3 percentage points) |
| Month 9 | MRR under ₹1,50,000 with no clear channel producing more than 30% of new logos |

Pivot options: (a) sell Room QR as an add-on through PMS vendors' marketplaces; (b) focus only on homestay clusters with a state tourism partnership; (c) fold into DineLoop as a "hotel restaurant" plan.
