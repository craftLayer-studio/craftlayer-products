## Trends 2024–2026

1. **GST cut to 5% without ITC on rooms up to ₹7,500** from 22 September 2025 (56th GST Council) ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/impact-of-gst-hospitality-industry)). Cheaper for guests, but owners lose input credit; FHRAI and HRAWI are lobbying to restore ITC ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/hrawi-proposes-5-percent-gst-hotel-room-rates-itc-benefit-125092401378_1.html)).
2. **Homestay formalisation**: NITI Aayog policy report, NIDHI+ registration, Airbnb–Ministry of Tourism MoU. More owners entering, needing simple tools.
3. **Supply growth**: about 70,000 branded rooms to be added by 2030 ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/04/15/india-to-add-70000-hotel-rooms-by-2030-in-hospitality-surge/)); branded competition pushes independents to sharpen guest experience and reviews.
4. **Direct booking push**: hotel tech vendors (STAAH, Bookingjini) report rising direct channel share; OTAs still dominate volume.
5. **WhatsApp as the guest channel**: per-message pricing since 1 July 2025; India marketing about ₹0.86 and utility about ₹0.115 per message ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). Utility messages (confirmations, check-in links) are cheap; marketing must be targeted.
6. **Contactless and QR habits** from 2020 onward are now normal for Indian guests.

## Owner pain points (ranked, from sources plus estimate)

1. OTA commission and parity pressure (sourced above).
2. Staff shortage and night coverage at homestays **(estimate, from typical owner-run operations)**.
3. Review score and OTA ranking anxiety **(estimate)**.
4. Compliance paperwork: guest register, Form C (sourced above).
5. Margin squeeze from GST ITC loss (sourced above).
