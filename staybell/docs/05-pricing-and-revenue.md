# StayBell — pricing and revenue

USD figures use ₹88 per USD **(estimate)**. Competitor anchors come from [04-market-research.md](04-market-research.md) (accessed 2026-11-24).

## Pricing logic

| Anchor | Price | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Desktell (QR guest service only) | ₹199 per room per month, so ₹2,985 for 15 rooms | QR-only tools charge per room; we undercut on total cost |
| eZee Absolute (PMS entry) | From about ₹2,250 | Owners already accept around ₹2,000–₹2,500 per month for core software |
| Djubo (PMS + channel manager, 10–20 rooms) | $50–$180 per month annual (about ₹4,400–₹15,800) | Upper bound for small-property software spend |
| Little Hotelier Basics | $39 per month | International anchor |
| Value created | About ₹14,600 per month OTA commission saved if 10% of a 15-room property's OTA nights shift direct **(estimate)** | Price should be under 20% of value created |

Rule: Room QR is priced below the cheapest PMS so it is an easy yes; Direct is priced near eZee's entry plan because it carries the commission-saving story.

**(estimate)**: occupancy, ADR and OTA share are illustrative. OTA commission = rooms × 30 × occupancy × OTA share × ADR × 18%. Savings exclude F&B and upsell gains.
