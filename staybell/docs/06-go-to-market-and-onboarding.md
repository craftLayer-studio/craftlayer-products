# StayBell — go-to-market and onboarding

## Launch strategy in one paragraph

Pick 2 dense leisure clusters (for example Rishikesh–Haridwar and Coorg–Chikmagalur, or one hill and one beach cluster near the founder). Each cluster has 100+ small properties within a 20 km radius **(estimate)**, owners talk to each other, and OTA commission anger is high. Win 3 free pilots per cluster, turn them into case studies with real numbers, then sell door-to-door with those numbers. Add homestay associations and state tourism homestay programmes as partners.

|---|---|---|---|

| 8 | Close remaining with "founding customer" offer: annual Direct plan at ₹19,990 (first 20 only) | 10 paying | Week 8–10 |

## Demo script (10 minutes)

1. **Hook (1 minute):** "How much did you pay MakeMyTrip and Booking.com last month?" Write the number down. "If even 10% of those guests booked you directly next time, you keep about ₹X."
2. **Room QR (3 minutes):** Hand the owner a sample standee. Owner scans; sees menu, towels, complaint button. Order a tea; the owner's phone gets the ticket.
3. **Check-in (2 minutes):** Fill the guest form as a foreign guest, upload a sample passport; show Form C data pack in portal order.
4. **CRM (2 minutes):** Show guest list growing; show a sample "come back direct" WhatsApp campaign.
5. **Close (2 minutes):** "We set up everything and print standees free for 30 days. If you do not see value, we take them back."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I already have eZee / Djubo." | "Keep it. StayBell is what your guest sees in the room and what brings them back directly. It works beside your PMS." |
| "Guests will not scan QR codes." | "Guests scan QR to pay UPI every day. We put the Wi-Fi password behind the scan; in pilots most rooms scan in the first week." (Replace with real pilot number.) |
| "OTA will punish me if I sell cheaper direct." | "Do not sell cheaper. Give perks: free breakfast, late checkout. Parity is about price, not perks." |
| "Too expensive." | "One direct booking a month covers Room QR. Your OTA bill last month was ₹X." |
| "My staff are not tech-savvy." | "Staff only tap 'Done' on a ticket. We train them in 20 minutes, in Hindi or the local language." |
| "Guest data privacy?" | "Notice and consent are built in, ID images are encrypted and deleted after the period you set. Better than photocopies in a drawer." |
| "It is off-season." | "Start now so your guest list is ready for the season. Off-season pause is ₹299 a month." |

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| No QR scans for 3 days | Call owner; check standees are in rooms; re-train staff |
| Owner not logged in for 7 days | Send weekly summary with one highlighted win |
| Fewer than 50% check-ins digital | Desk script: "Please scan to check in; it takes 1 minute" |
| Low review score month | Share complaint categories and fixes |
| Season ending | Offer pause plan instead of cancel |
| 200 consented guests reached | Trigger upgrade conversation to Direct |
| Month 3 on monthly plan | Offer annual with 2 months free |

| Funnel stage | Metric |
